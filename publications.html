Publications

Are We Done Yet?: A Vision-Based Judge for Autonomous Task Completion of Computer Use Agents

Authors: Marta Sumyk, Oleksandr Kosovan

Accepted at AAAI 2026 Workshop on Trust and Control in Agentic AI (TrustAgent)

This project studies whether Vision-Language Models can judge task completion for Computer Use Agents directly from screenshots and task descriptions. It introduces a dataset of 1,260 human-labeled tasks across 42 built-in macOS applications and shows that evaluator feedback can improve agent reliability and self-correction.

CUAAudit: Meta-Evaluation of Vision-Language Models as Auditors of Autonomous Computer-Use Agents

Authors: Marta Sumyk, Oleksandr Kosovan

Accepted at HEAL @ CHI 2026 Workshop on Human-centered Evaluation and Auditing of Language Models

This project evaluates Vision-Language Models as autonomous auditors of Computer Use Agents across macOS, Windows, and Linux benchmarks. It studies auditor performance in terms of accuracy, calibration, and inter-model agreement, highlighting both the promise and the limitations of model-based task evaluation.

Toward Agentic RAG for Ukrainian

Authors: Marta Sumyk, Oleksandr Kosovan

Research report for the UNLP 2026 Shared Task on Multi-Domain Document Understanding

This project investigates Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Ukrainian. The system combines two-stage retrieval with a lightweight agentic layer for query rephrasing and answer-retry loops. The paper shows that retrieval quality remains the main bottleneck for offline agentic RAG pipelines.

GBEM-UA: Gender Bias Evaluation and Mitigation for Ukrainian Large Language Models

Authors: Mykhailo Buleshnyi, Maksym Buleshnyi, Marta Sumyk, Nazarii Drushchak

UNLP 2025, co-located with ACL 2025

In a team of three, developed a benchmark for measuring gender bias in the hiring problem within the Ukrainian language. Researched four debiasing methods: prompting, soft debiasing, hard debiasing, and LoRA fine-tuning.

Screen2AX: Vision-Based Approach for Automatic macOS Accessibility Generation

Authors: Viktor Muryn, Marta Sumyk, Mariya Hirna, Sofiya Garkot, Maksym Shamrai

Developed a vision-only pipeline for reconstructing accessibility trees of macOS applications. The approach outperformed the current screen-grounding baseline.
