Synthetic Data Augmentation for Satellite-Based Analysis of Battle-Damaged Agricultural Fields in Ukraine

Marta Sumyk, Oleksandr Kosovan, Iryna Voitsitska

2026

arXiv preprint

Trains class-conditional GAN and DDPM generators on real satellite imagery to augment scarce, imbalanced training data for classifying battle-damaged agricultural fields; the best (DDPM) augmentation lifts a Vision Transformer classifier's balanced accuracy from 67% to 81% and recall on the underrepresented not-bombed class from 41% to 69%.

arXiv

Reinforcement Learning for Computer-Use Agents with Autonomous Evaluation

Marta Sumyk, Oleksandr Kosovan

2026

GLOW @ IJCAI 2026 (4th Workshop on Generalizing from Limited Resources in the Open World)

Models autonomous vision-language evaluator feedback as a noisy reward channel and derives a noise-corrected estimator for PPO fine-tuning of GUI agents, improving success rates by 12.6 points over zero-shot and 5.1 points over raw evaluator rewards across macOSWorld, Windows Agent Arena, and OSWorld.

Layer-Resolved Optimal Transport for Hallucination Detection in NMT and Abstractive Summarization

Mariia Onyshchuk, Maksym-Vasyl Tarnavskyi, Marta Sumyk

2026

ICML 2026 Mechanistic Interpretability Workshop

Extends optimal-transport hallucination detection across all decoder layers of an NMT model and tests whether the geometric signal transfers to abstractive-summarization faithfulness detection, mapping where the unsupervised signal holds and where it breaks down.

Toward Agentic RAG for Ukrainian

Marta Sumyk, Oleksandr Kosovan

2026

Research report, UNLP 2026 Shared Task on Multi-Domain Document Understanding

Combines two-stage retrieval (BGE-M3 with BGE reranking) with query-rephrasing and answer-retry loops on Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct for an offline Ukrainian RAG pipeline, finding retrieval quality — not agentic reasoning — is the binding bottleneck on score.

CUAAudit: Meta-Evaluation of Vision-Language Models as Auditors of Autonomous Computer-Use Agents

Marta Sumyk, Oleksandr Kosovan

2026

HEAL @ CHI 2026 (Workshop on Human-Centered Evaluation and Auditing of Language Models)

Meta-evaluates five vision-language models as autonomous auditors of computer-use-agent task completion across macOS, Windows, and Linux benchmarks; auditors reach strong accuracy and calibration overall but show notable disagreement with each other even when individually high-performing.

“Are We Done Yet?”: A Vision-Based Judge for Autonomous Task Completion of Computer Use Agents

Marta Sumyk, Oleksandr Kosovan

2026

TrustAgent @ AAAI 2026 (Workshop on Trust and Control in Agentic AI)

Presents a screenshot-based evaluator for computer-use agents and a dataset of 1,260 human-labeled tasks across 42 built-in macOS applications; the judge reaches 73% accuracy in detecting task success and lifts overall agent success by 27% (relative) when its feedback is applied.

Screen2AX: Vision-Based Approach for Automatic macOS Accessibility Generation

Viktor Muryn, Marta Sumyk, Mariya Hirna, Sofiya Garkot, Maksym Shamrai

2025

arXiv preprint

Reconstructs real-time, tree-structured macOS accessibility metadata directly from a screenshot with a 79% F1 score, using three newly released datasets spanning 302 macOS apps; improves GUI-grounding accuracy 2.2× over native accessibility metadata and outperforms OmniParser V2 on ScreenSpot.

GBEM-UA: Gender Bias Evaluation and Mitigation for Ukrainian Large Language Models

Mykhailo Buleshnyi, Maksym Buleshnyi, Marta Sumyk, Nazarii Drushchak

2025

Proceedings of the Fourth Ukrainian Natural Language Processing Workshop (UNLP 2025), pp. 64–72

Introduces a Ukrainian-language benchmark for gender bias in hiring that accounts for the language's grammatical gender and feminitives, and compares prompt, embedding, and fine-tuning debiasing — finding embedding debiasing alone insufficient for a morphologically rich language like Ukrainian.
